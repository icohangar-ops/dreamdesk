SEG1|What if an AI trading desk showed you every thought it had? This is DreamDesk: a multi-agent trading desk for dreamDEX Event Contracts, where every decision is signed, hashed, and auditable, in real time.
SEG2|dreamDEX Event Contracts are fully on-chain binary markets on Somnia. Will Bitcoin close this five minute window up, or down? Fixed payouts, zero fees, instant settlement. A perfect arena for autonomous agents — if, and only if, you can verify what the agent is actually doing.
SEG3|DreamDesk's answer is a pipeline you can inspect end to end. Three quantitative agents read live oracle prices: momentum, volatility, and sentiment. Their evidence goes to an LLM council of three jurors — trend, contrarian, and sentinel — who debate and vote. Consensus then passes eight deterministic risk gates before a single order is placed.
SEG4|Here is the trading floor, live on Somnia's Shannon testnet. The desk streams real oracle prices, discovers real on-chain markets, and quotes real order books. Watch a full decision cycle: agents score the tape, the council convenes, jurors vote with written rationales, and the risk gates arbitrate. When quorum, confidence, and edge all line up — the desk fires.
SEG5|Every step lands in a hash-chained audit ledger. Each event commits the hash of the one before it, so tampering with history is mathematically detectable. And the chain re-verifies itself, live, right here in the UI. When a window closes, the contract resolves on chain, and the settlement lands in the ledger, like everything else.
SEG6|The numbers: fourteen unit checks green — fills, payouts, and ledger tamper detection. Live end-to-end runs with real settlements on testnet. Paper and live modes share the exact same execution interface, so the demo you are watching is the code that trades.
